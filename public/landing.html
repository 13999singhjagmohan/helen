Questions

Is Helen a real person?

No. Every companion is fictional and AI-driven. They will never claim to be a real, identifiable person.

Who can use Helen?

Adults aged 18 or over, where adult content is legal. You confirm your age before entering.

Can I change what my companion remembers?

Yes. Open Memories in any chat to edit or delete anything, or reset the conversation completely.

What if a conversation goes somewhere I don't like?

Say your safe word or tap Stop. The companion stops immediately and checks on you.
